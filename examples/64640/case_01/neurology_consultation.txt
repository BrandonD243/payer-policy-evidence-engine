CHIEF COMPLAINT
Chronic posterior head pain.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS
The patient is a 48-year-old female presenting with severe pain located in the posterior scalp and upper neck region. Pain radiates along the distribution of the greater occipital nerve. Symptoms are consistent with occipital neuralgia and have been present for several months.

PHYSICAL EXAM
Marked tenderness over the greater occipital nerve distribution. Reproduction of pain with palpation.

ASSESSMENT
Occipital neuralgia.

PLAN
Recommend diagnostic occipital nerve block.
